**🎮 Godot 4 2D Platformer Game** is the complete English-language guide to building a 2D platformer in Godot 4. Learn player movement, jump physics, tilemaps, enemies, checkpoints, and level transitions — with runnable GDScript and a clean scene structure. Built for Godot beginners, indie developers, and anyone who wants a real 2D platformer foundation. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
